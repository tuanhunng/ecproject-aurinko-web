The Story

The first light of the day

Aurinko means “sun” in Finnish. We began with a single question: why must a beautiful watch be loud?

When the first light of the day bathed the quiet valley, the master watchmaker smiled as the dawn—known as Aurinko in Finnish—warmed his hands. Driven by a single, profound question—why must a beautiful watch be loud?—he chose to capture that soft, golden silence beneath a crystal dial. Now, every sweep of its hands is not a ticking rush, but a luxurious whisper reminding the wearer to cherish the present.

Policies and Customer Service

Policies and customer service

Purchase policy

  • Pay by bank transfer, card or cash on delivery.
  • Nationwide delivery within 1–3 days, in a gift box.
  • Returns within 14 days if the watch is unworn and tags are intact.

Warranty

  • 3-year movement warranty from the date of purchase.
  • Free repair of manufacturing defects.
  • Not covered: impact damage, water ingress beyond the rated resistance, or unauthorised opening of the case.

Care guide

  • Wipe with a soft dry cloth; avoid chemicals and perfume.
  • Do not press the crown when the watch is wet or underwater.
  • Have mechanical watches serviced and re-oiled every 3–5 years.
